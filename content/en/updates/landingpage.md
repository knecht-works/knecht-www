## Why this page

It is a risk to build a full product first and then hope that somebody needs it. Thus the landing page comes first. The page does three things:

- It explains the idea in a few sentences.
- It collects the first beta testers.
- It makes the path public, so that feedback comes early and not only at launch.

### Language

Many startups aim at the international market from the start. We think that this starts with the language. We will focus on the DACH region first. Thus English as a second language must wait a little longer.

## What the page is not (yet)

We want to prevent false expectations. This page is not a product. It has no login, no demo, and nothing that you can try. You see the idea and a first look at the planned interface.

## What comes next

The prototype comes next, with projects, workflows, and the AI agent.

The goal for the next weeks is clear. We want to find the first 3 to 5 agencies as beta testers. They test Knecht on real projects and give honest feedback about what works.
