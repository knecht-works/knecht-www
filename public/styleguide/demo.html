styleguide

Knecht Styleguide

Shared styles, assets and JS for Knecht test fixtures. Defined once, served globally from knecht.works, imported by every fixture with two lines.

View kit.css

Add it to a fixture

Drop these two lines into the fixture's <head>:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://knecht.works/styleguide/kit.css">
<script src="https://knecht.works/styleguide/kit.js" defer></script>
Served globally from knecht.works · MIT
